Minecraft Meets Path of Exile & Diablo!

Craft to Exile is a modpack designed around progression. Mods in Craft to Exile aim to enhance the players' experience with Mine and Slash - a thorough ARPG mod. Mine and Slash draws inspiration from popular action RPGs such as Diablo and Path of Exile.

Craft to Exile has a focus on progression with more dimensions becoming available as you increase your level.

Featuring:
- Over 250 quests!
- Over 100 custom items (uniques, sets, runewords).
- Thousands of lines of configuration via datapacks and CraftTweaker.
- An official server provided by Akliz!

Mods:
- Mine and Slash - turns Minecraft into an ARPG!
- The Bumblezone, Endergetic Expansion, Atum 2: Return to the Sands, Good Night's Sleep, Tropicraft, Midnight, Gaia Dimension, and Dimensional Dungeons.
- FTB Quests - with fully developed quest log.
- Traverse: Legacy, Valhelsia Structures, Dungeon Crawl, Dungeons Plus - for fun world gen.
- Create and Immersive Engineering - fully integrated into the quest log and with each other.
- MineColonies, Simple Farming, Pam's HarvestCraft 2, Aquaculture 2.
- Spice of Life: Carrot Edition - fully integrated with Mine and Slash!
- A unique summoning system from a stripped-down Lycanites Mobs - summon 2 of 6 primal elementals to fuse them into stronger para-elementals!
- And many more!

Not sure what CTE2 is?

Craft to Exile 2 is an action-RPG modpack inspired by Path of Exile and Diablo. The pack focuses on player expression and guided progression. It brings:

- Revamped Combat
- Extensive Character Customization (Classes, Talent Tree, Ascendancies, Equipment)
- Custom Weapons, Armors, Mobs, Bosses
- End-Game Content (Atlas System, Maps, Uber & Pinnacle Bosses, Twilight Forest)
- Overhauled Dimensions
- Over 650 Quests with a full-on 5 Act Campaign!
- VR Support
